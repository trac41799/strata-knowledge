**AGENTS.md clauses activated:** §1 (orient), §2 (validate), §5 (cite honestly), §7 (debrief).

```
I want to validate my understanding of <topic id or title>.

My claim: <state your claim, including the reasoning you believe supports it>

Follow AGENTS.md. Resolve the topic, read its concept.md, and return:
1. A verdict: [correct | partial | incorrect]
2. The evidence tiers involved and the S-#### record citations
```

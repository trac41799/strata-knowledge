Introduces metacognition — knowledge and monitoring of one's own cognition.
Basis for Strata's calibration system: learners predict performance before
validation, agents record predicted-vs-actual, and both reflect on the gap
(`calibration.updated` event; metacognitive prompt in debriefs).

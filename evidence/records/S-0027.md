Controlled benchmark methodology comparing precise tracing garbage collection
against malloc/free on identical programs. With 5x the memory explicit
management requires, a generational tracing collector matches or slightly
exceeds it (up to 9% faster); at 3x it runs ~17% slower, at 2x ~70% slower,
and under physical-memory scarcity paging makes GC an order of magnitude
slower. Quantifies the time-space tradeoff: generational collectors add up to
~50% space and 5-10% runtime overhead when memory is not scarce.

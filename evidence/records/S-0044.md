Introduces natural deduction, with one introduction and one elimination rule
for each connective and quantifier, and the sequent calculus (LJ/LK) with the
cut rule; proves the Hauptsatz (cut-elimination): every LK derivation can be
transformed into a normal, cut-free derivation, yielding consistency of the
calculus. Defines the classical vs intuitionistic systems by the availability
of the law of excluded middle (equivalently double-negation elimination).
Basis for the natural deduction and proof-strategy claims of this topic.

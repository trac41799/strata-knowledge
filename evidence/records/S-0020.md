Organizational Project-Enabling (6), Technical Management (8), Technical (14);
30 processes total. NOTE: superseded by ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2026 (published 2026-04-15), which
harmonizes the process model with ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2015 and renames a
requirements process. Claims citing S-0020 are dated to the 2017 edition.

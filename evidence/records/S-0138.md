The original statement of Conway's law: "organizations which design systems
are constrained to produce designs which are copies of the communication
structures of these organizations." Conway presents the relationship as an
empirical constraint observed in the systems his design committees produced,
not as a recommendation. Widely adopted in practitioner literature on module
boundaries, team topology, and architecture. Web-verified: reprint hosted on
the author's site (melconway.com), Datamation, April 1968, 14(4), 28-31;
Wikipedia cites Datamation 14(5):28-31 — the month and page range (April
1968, pp. 28-31) are consistent across sources.

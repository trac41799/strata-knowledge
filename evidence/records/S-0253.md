Open, community-led maturity model (CC BY-SA 4.0) for formulating and
implementing a software-security strategy integrated into an existing SDLC.
Defines five business functions — Governance, Design, Implementation,
Verification, Operations — with three security practices each (15 practices,
30 streams), assessed at maturity levels 0–3. Verified from owaspsamm.org:
e.g., the Security Testing practice pairs stream A "Scalable Baseline"
(automated security testing tools, integrated into build and deploy) with
stream B "Deep Understanding" (manual testing of high-risk components,
penetration testing); the model also names Misuse/Abuse Testing under
Requirements-driven Testing and Policy & Standards under Policy & Compliance.

Systematic mapping study of the monolith-to-microservices migration literature
(157 references). Findings: scalability and maintenance are the main stated
drivers of migration, but few studies actually assess whether those benefits
materialize; key challenges are service communication and database migration;
most research focuses on monolith decomposition. The state of the evidence
base is thin — the literature maps techniques rather than demonstrating
outcomes. Web-verified: ScienceDirect listing (Information and Software
Technology, Volume 177, article 107590; online October 2024, in-press
January 2025) with the four authors and the abstract quoted above.

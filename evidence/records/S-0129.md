Practitioner lore originating at Sun Microsystems: L. Peter Deutsch drafted
the first seven fallacies in 1994 (building on four identified earlier by
Bill Joy and Dave Lyon), and James Gosling added the eighth around 1997. The
fallacies: the network is reliable; latency is zero; bandwidth is infinite;
the network is secure; topology does not change; there is one administrator;
transport cost is zero; the network is homogeneous. Widely adopted in
distributed-systems education and industry literature (Deutsch discusses
their history and scope in SE Radio episode 470, 2021). Basis for the T3
fallacies claims of `systems-software/distributed-systems-basics`.

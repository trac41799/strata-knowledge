The System R optimizer paper: how a relational DBMS chooses access
paths for single-relation and join queries given a boolean predicate
specification. Introduces the cost-based architecture still used by
mainstream optimizers: catalog statistics (relation cardinality,
distinct-key counts, selectivity factors), dynamic-programming
enumeration of join orders, estimation of intermediate cardinalities
as products of relation sizes and predicate selectivity, and cost
models for nested-loop joins and index/sequential scans. Web-verified:
ACM Digital Library DOI 10.1145/582095.582099; DBLP SIGMOD 1979 pp.
23-34.

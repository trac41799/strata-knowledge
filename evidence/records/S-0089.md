Codified consensus (Internet Standard) defining the Domain Name System:
the hierarchical tree name space rooted at ".", zones and authoritative
name servers, resource records with TTL-based caching, and recursive vs
non-recursive resolution (RD/RA). Companion to RFC 1035 (implementation
and specification). Authoritative source for DNS claims in
`systems-software/networking-basics`.

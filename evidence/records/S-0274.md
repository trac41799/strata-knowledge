InstructGPT: three-stage RLHF — (1) supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on labeler
demonstrations of desired behavior; (2) a reward model (RM) trained on human
rankings of sampled outputs; (3) policy optimization with PPO against the RM
(plus a 10% pretraining-data mix, "PPO-ptx"). In human evaluations, outputs
from the 1.3B-parameter InstructGPT were preferred over outputs from the 175B
GPT-3, and the 175B InstructGPT was preferred over 175B GPT-3 ~85% of the time
(85 +/- 3%). InstructGPT was rated truthful/informative roughly twice as often
as GPT-3, hallucination dropped from 41% to 21% on summarization and
closed-domain QA, and toxic outputs decreased, with only small regressions on
public NLP benchmarks ("alignment tax"). Verified against arXiv:2203.02155
abstract and detailed reviews.

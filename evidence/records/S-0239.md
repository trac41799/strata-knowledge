A globally accessible knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques based
on real-world observations, organized as a matrix (e.g., Enterprise: 14
tactics from Reconnaissance to Impact, each tactic with techniques,
sub-techniques, procedures, detections, and mitigations). Used as a foundation
for developing threat models and methodologies in the private sector,
government, and security product community. Authoritative source for the
ATT&CK claims of the `security/threat-modeling` topic.

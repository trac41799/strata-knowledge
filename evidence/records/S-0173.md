Companion workbook to the SRE book, providing the hands-on practice chapters.
Web-verified at sre.google/workbook/on-call (Chapter 8, "Being On-Call"): being
on-call means being available during a set period of time and being ready to
respond to production incidents with appropriate urgency; during on-call shifts
SREs diagnose, mitigate, fix, or escalate incidents as needed; playbooks contain
high-level instructions on how to respond to automated alerts, explain the
severity and impact of the alert, and include debugging suggestions and possible
actions to mitigate impact and fully resolve the alert; "in SRE, whenever an
alert is created, a corresponding playbook entry is usually created"; playbooks
"reduce stress, the mean time to repair (MTTR), and the risk of human error."
Basis for the on-call, playbook, severity-classification, and playbooks-reduce-
MTTR claims of `operations/incident-response`.

Canonical practitioner treatment of the delivery pipeline: an automated
sequence of stages (commit, acceptance, capacity, manual, production) that
raises release confidence as a change progresses from check-in to release.
Covers continuous integration, configuration management, testing strategy,
zero-downtime releases and rolling back database changes, and components and
dependencies. The origin of blue-green deployment is documented to Dan North
and Jez Humble (c. 2005), during the work that became this book: deploy the
new version into a parallel environment, test it there, then switch
production traffic; rollback is instant re-routing to the previous
environment.

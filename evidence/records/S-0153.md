View-model paper describing software architecture through multiple concurrent
views: logical (object model), process (concurrency and synchronization),
development (code organization), and physical (deployment mapping), plus a
scenario view (the "+1") used to illustrate and validate the architecture.
Each view addresses the concerns of a stakeholder group (end users, developers,
systems engineers, project managers), and functional and non-functional
requirements are handled separately. Web-verified: arXiv copy of the paper
(2006.04975); Wikipedia and philippe.kruchten.com confirm the citation (IEEE
Software 12(6):42-50, DOI 10.1109/52.469759).

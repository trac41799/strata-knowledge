Tutorial/history of the working set model by its originator: programs
cluster references into small subsets for extended periods (locality),
locality was experimentally validated over a decade of studies, reference
behavior exhibits phases and transitions (semi-Markov phase structure), and
working-set memory management is near-optimal among non-lookahead policies.
Established the empirical basis for caching and virtual memory design.

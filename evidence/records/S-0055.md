Canonical quantitative treatment of fundamental data structures and algorithms:
binary search trees and balanced trees (§6.2), hashing with chaining and open
addressing and the load-factor analysis of probe counts (§6.4), heaps and
heapsort (§5.2.3), and the decision-tree lower bound for comparison sorting
(§5.3.1), with asymptotic (Big-O) analysis throughout. Primary source for the
complexity claims of `cs-foundations/data-structures`.

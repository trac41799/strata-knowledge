Codified consensus of the computing profession's ethical commitments, adopted
by the ACM Council on June 22, 2018. Structure: preamble plus four sections —
1. General Ethical Principles (1.1-1.7: contribute to society, avoid harm,
be honest and trustworthy, be fair and not discriminate, respect work behind
new ideas/inventions/creative works, respect privacy, honor confidentiality);
2. Professional Responsibilities (2.1-2.9: quality, competence, know and
respect rules, professional review, comprehensive evaluation including risk
analysis, work only in areas of competence, public awareness, authorized
access only, robustly and usably secure systems); 3. Professional Leadership
Principles (public good as central concern, social responsibilities, care
with systems integrated into society's infrastructure); 4. Compliance with
the Code (uphold and promote the code, express concern about breaches, treat
violations as inconsistent with ACM membership). Commitment to ethical
conduct is required of every ACM member, SIG member, and award recipient.

Proves that in a totally asynchronous model of computation every deterministic
consensus protocol has the possibility of nontermination, even with only one
faulty process; by contrast, solutions are known for the synchronous case (the
Byzantine Generals problem). The paper concludes the result does not show the
problem cannot be "solved" in practice — it motivates refined models with
realistic timing assumptions and relaxed requirements.

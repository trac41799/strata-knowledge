The original widely-read description of the UNIX process model: the fork()
system call creates a child process that is a copy of the parent, exec()
replaces the calling process's image with a new program, and the two are
separate operations — the mechanism behind starting asynchronous processes
and the shell. Received the ACM Best Paper Award (systems and programming
languages) in 1974.

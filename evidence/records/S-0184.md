The formal paper on 3NF synthesis. Presents an effective procedure
(the synthesis algorithm) that converts a set of functional
dependencies into a database schema, and PROVES the resulting schema
is in third normal form, preserves all functional dependencies, and
has the lossless-join property. Also formalizes the inference rules
for functional dependencies used to compute closures and keys.

Early hierarchical quality model: top-level characteristics (as-is
utility, maintainability, portability) decomposed into second-level
attributes and finally into primitive source-code characteristics. With
McCall et al. (1977), established the characteristic-decomposition pattern
later standardized by ISO 9126 and SQuaRE.

Five-stage skill-acquisition model (novice → advanced beginner → competent →
proficient → expert) with distinct rule-based vs intuitive decision modes.
Basis for Strata's per-track learner levels and for matching validation
scenarios (rule-following at early stages, judgment at later stages).

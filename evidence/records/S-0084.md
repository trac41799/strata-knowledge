Introduces the spiral model: a risk-driven, iterative lifecycle in which
each cycle covers objective setting, risk assessment and reduction (e.g.,
prototyping), development and verification, and planning of the next
cycle; aimed at large, complex projects where risk dominates. Republished
in IEEE Computer 21(5), 1988 (doi: 10.1109/2.59).

Codified consensus (U.S. federal standard) specifying AES, the symmetric block
cipher selected via the NIST competition (Rijndael, Daemen & Rijmen). Specifies
AES-128/192/256 with 128-bit blocks: encryption/decryption transforms, key
schedule, and block/state structure. Authoritative source for the
symmetric-encryption claims of the `security/cryptography-basics` topic.

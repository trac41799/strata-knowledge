Historical review showing iterative and incremental development (IID) has
roots in the mid-1950s, long before the agile movement. Documented IID
approaches varied from significant up-front specification with time-boxed
incremental development to strongly evolutionary, feedback-driven
approaches, but all shared the aim of avoiding a single-pass, sequential,
document-driven, gated-step process.

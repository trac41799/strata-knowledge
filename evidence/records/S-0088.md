Codified consensus (Internet Standard) defining the Internet architecture
and host requirements for the link, internet, and transport layers: the
four-layer protocol suite (application, transport, internet, link),
IP as a connectionless datagram service with 32-bit addresses, TCP/UDP
behavior, well-known ports (RFC 793 Section 2.7), Ethernet/IEEE 802
encapsulation and ARP, and the robustness principle. Authoritative source
for the `systems-software/networking-basics` topic.

The most widely used introductory cryptography textbook; emphasizes formal
definitions, precise assumptions, and rigorous proofs of security. Covers the
content of the `security/cryptography-basics` topic: private-key (symmetric)
encryption, message authentication codes (MACs, HMAC), hash functions and
collision resistance (incl. the birthday bound), block cipher modes and
authenticated encryption (GCM), public-key encryption (RSA and RSA-OAEP),
elliptic-curve cryptography, Diffie-Hellman key exchange, Kerckhoffs's
principle, and the practice rule against designing one's own cryptosystems.

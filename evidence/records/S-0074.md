Canonical, widely-cited RE roadmap (ICSE 2000 Future of Software Engineering
track). Frames requirements engineering as interleaved activities —
elicitation, modelling and analysis, communication and agreement, and
evolution — recurring across the life cycle rather than a single upfront
phase. Surveys elicitation technique families (traditional, observational,
model-based, exploratory), notes prototyping for high-uncertainty situations,
and characterizes requirements problems as "wicked": ill-defined boundaries,
dynamic, embedded in organizational contexts, and prone to stakeholder
conflict. Managing change is a fundamental RE activity (impact analysis,
configuration management).

Canonical practitioner source for SRE practice. Chapter 4 (Service Level
Objectives) defines SLIs, SLOs, and SLAs and introduces error budgets.
Chapter 6 (Monitoring Distributed Systems) defines the four golden signals —
latency, traffic, errors, saturation — states that monitoring serves
alerting, dashboards, and retrospective analysis, and codifies the alerting
philosophy: alerts are for "something is broken, and somebody needs to fix
it right now," with checklist questions designed to avoid false positives
and pager burnout. Basis for SLO/error-budget and alerting claims.

Covers SLI/SLO/SLA (ch. 4), golden signals and alerting (ch. 6),
postmortems, incident roles/on-call, escalation, and MTTR framing.

Practitioner guide to the canonical caching patterns and their trade-offs:
cache-aside (the application is responsible for the cache-orchestration flow;
load-on-miss from the data store); read-through (the cache loads missing
values from the store itself; "solid default"); write-through (store and
cache updated together; "solid default"); write-behind/write-back
(asynchronous flush; choose when performance outweighs short-term
consistency; "asynchronous systems are harder to reason with"); refresh-ahead
(refresh hot entries before they expire); and cache invalidation/TTL planning
(entries stored until they expire, then refetched via cache-aside or
read-through).

Large-N observational study: 728 GitHub projects, 63 million SLOC, 29,000
authors, 1.5 million commits, 17 languages. Mixed-methods regression found
that language design has a "significant, but modest" association with software
quality; after controlling for project size, age, team, and domain, features
such as static vs dynamic typing and functional style explain only a small
share of defect differences. Authors and follow-up work caution against causal
readings. Basis for the T1 claims on paradigm tradeoffs.

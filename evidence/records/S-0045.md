First explicit axiomatization of the natural numbers, introducing the Peano
axioms: a first element (1 in the original), an injective successor function,
equality as a congruence, and the induction axiom — any property holding of
the first element and closed under successor holds of every natural number.
Also introduced the pervasive notations for set membership, subset, and set
operations. Basis for the mathematical-induction claims of this topic.

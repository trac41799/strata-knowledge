Canonical citation for the waterfall model: documents the sequential
stage scheme (requirements, design, implementation, testing, operation)
with feedback between stages. The paper is widely misread as endorsing a
rigid one-pass process; Royce actually argued the sequential scheme is
risky and recommended feedback, iteration between stages, and building a
pilot version before final construction.

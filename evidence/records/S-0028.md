Canonical reference covering the full collector design space: motivation
(dangling pointers, double frees, leaks), roots and reachability, mark-sweep,
mark-compact, copying/semi-space, generational collection (hypothesis,
remembered sets, write barriers), reference counting and its cycle problem,
fragmentation, and parallel, incremental, concurrent and real-time collectors
including modern low-pause designs (C4, Shenandoah, ZGC). Basis for the
practice-level (T3) claims of this topic.

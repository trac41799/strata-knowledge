Codified consensus product quality model: 9 characteristics (Functional
Suitability, Performance Efficiency, Compatibility, Interaction Capability,
Security, Safety, Reliability, Maintainability, Flexibility) with
subcharacteristics. Basis for quality/tracking rubrics (`rubrics/`) and the
quality-testing track (`standards/iso25010-map.md`).

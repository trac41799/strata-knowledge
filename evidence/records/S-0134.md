Canonical computer-architecture textbook (2017 ACM A.M. Turing Award
authors). Chapter 4 "Data-Level Parallelism in Vector, SIMD, and GPU
Architectures" covers vector architectures, SIMD instruction-set extensions,
and graphics processing units (SIMT execution, threads/warps) and the
DLP-vs-TLP taxonomy ("many parallel processors are hybrids" of SIMD and
MIMD); Chapter 5 "Multiprocessors and Thread-Level Parallelism" covers
symmetric shared-memory multiprocessors, performance (compulsory, false
sharing, and true sharing misses), spin locks and synchronization, and
models of memory consistency; Chapter 6 covers warehouse-scale computers.
Basis for the T3 taxonomy, parallelism-model, false-sharing, lock, and
GPU claims of `systems-software/parallel-programming`.

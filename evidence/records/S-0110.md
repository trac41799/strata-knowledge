Codified consensus measurement framework for SQuaRE: defines how to
develop and select quality measures, distinguishing quality measures on
internal properties (static product attributes) from measures on external
properties (dynamic behavior) and quality-in-use measures (outcomes in
context); applies to product quality, quality in use, data quality, and
IT service quality.

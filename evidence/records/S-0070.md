De-facto standard calling convention and data-layout ABI for x86-64 on
SysV platforms (Linux, macOS, BSD). Defines integer argument registers
(rdi, rsi, rdx, rcx, r8, r9; further args on the stack), return in rax
(rdx:rax for wide returns), floating-point args in xmm0-7, callee-saved
registers (rbx, rbp, r12-r15), 16-byte stack alignment at the call point,
and the 128-byte red zone below rsp.

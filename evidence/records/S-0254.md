The CVE Program provides shared identifiers — CVE records — for publicly
known cybersecurity vulnerabilities, acting as the backbone of the global
vulnerability-management ecosystem (advisories, NVD, scanners, incident
response). Verified via cve.org and MITRE's program anniversary release: grew
from 321 records in 1999 to 240,000+ by October 2024; 400+ CVE Numbering
Authorities (CNAs) from 40+ countries — vendors, open-source projects, and
coordination centers — assign and populate CVE records for products within
their scope; MITRE operates as Editor and Primary CNA. A CVE record identifies
and describes a disclosed vulnerability; remediation is tracked separately by
affected parties.

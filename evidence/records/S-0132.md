The paper that gave Amdahl's law its name: Amdahl argues that the fraction
of a computation that is inherently serial bounds the speedup obtainable
from parallelism. The argument is mathematical and yields the standard
bound: with serial fraction s and N processors, speedup is at most
1 / (s + (1-s)/N), approaching 1/s as N grows. The derivation assumes a
fixed-size problem (the serial portion s does not shrink as processors are
added). Basis for the T0 Amdahl's-law claims of
`systems-software/parallel-programming`.

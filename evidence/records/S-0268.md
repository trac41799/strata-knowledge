Verified publisher record (MIT Press, Nov 2016, 800 pp.): the standard
graduate-level textbook on deep learning, by three founding researchers.
Covers the mathematical foundations (linear algebra, probability, numerical
optimization), linear models and logistic regression, loss functions,
gradient-based optimization and its convergence behavior, the perceptron and
universal approximation properties of MLPs, activation functions, backpropagation
derivations, the vanishing/exploding gradient phenomenon and its mitigations
(initialization, ReLU, normalization, residual connections, gating), overfitting
and regularizers (weight decay, dropout, early stopping, data augmentation),
training dynamics (learning rate, batch size, adaptive optimizers such as
Adam/RMSProp), and the CNN and RNN families. Widely adopted as the canonical
reference text; the basis for established-practice (T3) claims in this pack.

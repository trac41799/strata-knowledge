Codified consensus (Internet Standard) defining IPv6 addressing: 128-bit
addresses as identifiers for interfaces and sets of interfaces, the
preferred hexadecimal text representation (x:x:x:x:x:x:x:x with "::"),
unicast/anycast/multicast address types, and the mandatory link-local
address. Authoritative source for IPv6 claims in
`systems-software/networking-basics`.

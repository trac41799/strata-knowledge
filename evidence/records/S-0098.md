Widely adopted graduate/undergraduate textbook covering the full paradigm
landscape: imperative vs declarative styles, control flow and subroutines,
object-oriented programming (encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism),
functional programming (purity, higher-order functions, evaluation strategy),
and logic programming, plus multi-paradigm languages (C++, Python, Ruby,
JavaScript) and their pragmatic tradeoffs. Basis for the practice-level (T3)
claims of the programming-paradigms topic.

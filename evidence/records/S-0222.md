Mixed-methods study of tool-based ("modern") code review at Microsoft:
observation, interviews, a survey of developers and managers, and manual
classification of hundreds of review comments across diverse teams.
Finding defect-related issues remains the main stated motivation for
review, but the measured outcomes are broader: knowledge transfer
(review as a learning tool), increased team awareness, and alternative or
better solutions (about 17% of surveyed developers ranked "alternative
solutions" as their first motivation). The paper also documents
challenges of the practice. Widely cited as the canonical empirical
study of modern code review's purposes and outcomes. Web-verified via
IEEE Xplore / TU Delft research portal / Microsoft Research preprint
(accessed 2026-08-19).

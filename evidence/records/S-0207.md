Widely adopted agile-practice book that introduced the "test automation
pyramid": a large base of unit tests, a middle layer of service (integration)
tests, and as few user-interface tests as possible at the top. Cohn's
rationale: UI tests are brittle, expensive to write, and time-consuming,
while unit tests are fast and cheap, so the pyramid guides where automated
testing effort should be invested. Web-verified: publisher/Amazon records
(ISBN 978-0-321-57936-2, Addison-Wesley, Oct 2009), Google Books entry, and
Martin Fowler's "The Practical Test Pyramid" article crediting Cohn's book
as the origin of the pyramid.

Empirical study introducing the Join Order Benchmark (JOB): a
real-world schema (IMDB) and 113 realistic multi-join queries.
Disentangles the contributions of cardinality estimation, plan
enumeration, and cost modeling across several mainstream optimizers.
Findings: cardinality estimation errors of several orders of
magnitude are ubiquitous; estimation error is the dominant cause of
bad plans (outweighing enumeration and cost-model quality); even with
perfect cardinality estimates, no tested optimizer reliably found the
optimal join order; poor join orders yield runtime degradations of
orders of magnitude. Web-verified: DOI 10.14778/2850583.2850594 per
Semantic Scholar and PVLDB.

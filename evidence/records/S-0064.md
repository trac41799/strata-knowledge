Standard undergraduate textbook (40,000+ students/year). Chapters 2 and 5
cover RISC-V ISA basics (32 GPRs, x0 hardwired zero, load-store design,
instruction formats, pseudo-instructions, assemblers), SRAM vs DRAM
technology, cache structure and write policies, and the memory hierarchy
pyramid. The "Real Stuff" sections discuss x86 (variable-length encoding,
internal micro-op translation) and ARMv8. ISBN 978-0-12-820331-6.

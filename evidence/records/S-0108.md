Foundational quality model (RADC final technical report): 11 quality
factors under three perspectives — product operation (correctness,
reliability, efficiency, integrity, usability), product revision
(maintainability, flexibility, testability), product transition
(portability, reusability, interoperability) — with factors explicitly
linked to criteria and metrics, establishing the decomposition pattern.

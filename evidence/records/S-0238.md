The canonical exposition of attack trees: an attack goal is the tree root and
is decomposed into sub-goals via AND/OR logic (alternative paths OR-ed,
required steps AND-ed), with leaves as concrete attacker actions; assigning
values such as cost, skill, or probability to nodes supports reasoning about
the cheapest or most likely attack path. Authoritative source for the
attack-tree claims of the `security/threat-modeling` topic.

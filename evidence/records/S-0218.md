Experience report on Google's continuous integration testing (TAP era,
~4.2M tests): approximately 1.5% of tests (~62,000) exhibited flaky behavior
in a given week; roughly 16% of tests show flakiness over time; flaky tests
were involved in 84% of cases where a stable passing test transitioned to
failing; re-running flaky tests consumed 2-16% of compute resources;
documented developer costs (triage effort, dismissal of legitimate failures
as flaky, delayed deploys). Widely cited industrial-scale evidence of flaky
test prevalence and its operational cost.

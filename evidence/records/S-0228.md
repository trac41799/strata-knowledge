Widely circulated Google tech-talk slide deck (the source of "Latency
Numbers Every Programmer Should Know"): order-of-magnitude latency
table — L1 cache 0.5 ns, L2 7 ns, main memory 100 ns, compress 1K Zippy
~10 us, send 2K over 1 Gbps ~20 us, same-datacenter round trip 0.5 ms,
disk seek 10 ms, read 1 MB sequentially from disk ~30 ms, CA-Netherlands
round trip ~150 ms — used for back-of-the-envelope design estimation
(e.g., serial 30-seek thumbnail page ~560 ms vs parallel ~18 ms). Practice
guidance: design for low latency by watching 90th/99th percentiles and
variance, not just the average; caching, pre-computation, and
parallelization as first-order design levers; estimate performance of a
design before building it. Practitioner source (T3): numbers are
order-of-magnitude and era-dependent (circa 2007-2013 hardware).

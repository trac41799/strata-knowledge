Introduces Raft, a consensus algorithm for replicated logs: strong leadership,
terms with randomized election timeouts, majority-based log commitment, and
safety under all non-Byzantine conditions (delays, partitions, packet loss,
duplication, reordering); servers fail by stopping and may recover. Also
documents Paxos's two-phase structure and its understandability problems,
with a student user study comparing Raft vs Paxos.

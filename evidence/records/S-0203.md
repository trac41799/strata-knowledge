Formal treatment of the transaction commit problem. Specifies atomic commit
precisely (all participants must agree on commit or abort; commit requires
unanimous willingness) and proves that commit protocols are consensus
problems: two-phase commit (2PC) is shown to be a special case of Paxos
consensus. The formal analysis grounds the classic blocking result: after a
participant has voted yes, the transaction manager's failure can leave that
participant unable to decide alone, so 2PC blocks until the coordinator
recovers; non-blocking atomic commit requires a majority-based protocol such
as Paxos Commit, which makes progress while a majority of coordinators are
working. Web-verified: author-hosted PDF (s2.smu.edu/~mhd/8330f11/p133-gray.pdf),
CMU 15-721 commit-protocol lecture slides, ACM DL record.

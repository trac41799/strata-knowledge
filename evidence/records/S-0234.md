Codified consensus (U.S. government guideline) surveying the NIST-approved
cryptographic mechanisms: symmetric block ciphers (AES per FIPS 197), approved
modes of operation (SP 800-38A ECB/CBC/CFB/OFB/CTR; GCM/GMAC per SP 800-38D as
authenticated encryption), approved hash functions (FIPS 180-4 SHA-2 and
FIPS 202 SHA-3), message authentication codes (HMAC, FIPS 198-1), and key
establishment (DH, RSA, ECC). Authoritative source for the approval-status
claims of the `security/cryptography-basics` topic.

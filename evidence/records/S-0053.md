Formalizes amortization as a general complexity technique: bounds the total
cost of a sequence of data-structure operations averaged over the sequence,
via accounting and potential-function methods. Establishes that doubling
dynamic arrays achieve O(1) amortized time per append and develops the
framework used to analyze rehashing and other resizing data structures.
Primary source for the amortized-complexity claims of
`cs-foundations/data-structures`.

Controlled experiment (Carleton University) comparing mutant detection with
detection of hand-seeded and real faults across eight subject programs,
including the SPACE program with real faults and the Siemens suite with
hand-seeded faults. Found the difficulty of detecting mutants statistically
similar to that of detecting real faults in the SPACE case, supporting
mutation testing as an appropriate proxy for real faults when comparing
testing techniques; in the hand-seeded-fault subjects, mutants tended to be
somewhat harder to detect than the seeded faults. Canonical empirical basis
for using mutation score as a test adequacy criterion.

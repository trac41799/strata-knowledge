Verified via publisher (NeurIPS 30) and arXiv records: introduces the
Transformer, a sequence-transduction architecture based entirely on
self-attention, dispensing with recurrence and convolution; attention layers
connect all positions with constant path length, enabling parallelization and
faster training than RNNs. Reports state-of-the-art BLEU on WMT translation
tasks. The paper's claim that the architecture removes the sequential
bottleneck and scales better is the founding evidence for the transformer as
the base architecture of modern sequence models and large language models
(established practice, T3).

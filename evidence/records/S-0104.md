Study of "optimization-unstable code": code whose correctness relies on
undefined behavior and that compiler optimizations therefore silently discard,
reorder, or transform. Found such code in real production systems, including
the Linux kernel and PostgreSQL, with concrete crash/security consequences.
Establishes empirically that UB in C/C++ is pervasive in practice and that its
effects are amplified by optimization. Basis for the T1 claims of the
memory-model-and-pointers topic.

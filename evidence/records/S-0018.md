Codified consensus undergraduate CS curriculum: 17 Knowledge Areas (e.g.,
Algorithmic Foundations, Mathematical and Statistical Foundations, Systems
Fundamentals), a knowledge-unit model (core/KA-core/elective), and a shift to a
competency model. Strata's CS-foundations spine and coverage-audit target
(`standards/cs2023-map.md`).

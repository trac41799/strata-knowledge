Codified consensus (Internet Standard) specifying HTTP/3: a mapping of
HTTP semantics onto the QUIC transport (RFC 9000), a UDP-based protocol
providing stream multiplexing, per-stream flow control, TLS 1.3 inside
the transport, and low-latency connection establishment. Authoritative
source for HTTP/3 claims in `systems-software/http-basics`.

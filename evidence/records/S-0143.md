Introduces GRASP — General Responsibility Assignment Software Patterns — a
learning aid of nine principles for assigning responsibilities to objects:
Information Expert, Creator, Controller, Low Coupling, High Cohesion,
Polymorphism, Pure Fabrication, Indirection, and Protected Variations. Larman
positions GRASP as complementary to the GoF catalog: GoF documents solution
structures, GRASP answers which object should hold which responsibility.
Web-verified: 2nd edition (2002) with GRASP chapter (O'Reilly catalog,
ISBN 0-13-092569-1); UCSB course excerpt states "nine fundamental principles
... organized in a learning aid called the GRASP patterns."

Large-scale study (ACM Distinguished Paper) of 100 test suites from five
open-source Java projects comprising up to 724,000 lines of code. The authors
measured statement, decision, and modified-condition coverage and used
mutation testing to evaluate each suite's fault-detection effectiveness. They
found a low-to-moderate correlation between coverage and effectiveness once
the number of test cases was controlled for, and that stronger coverage
criteria did not provide greater insight into effectiveness. Implication:
coverage is a poor predictor of test suite quality on its own — a suite can
achieve high coverage while testing little of substance. Web-verified: ACM DL
(DOI 10.1145/2568225.2568271), The Morning Paper (2014-10-21), and
neverworkintheory.org summary with abstract quote.

Canonical definition of the microservices architectural style: developing a
single application as a suite of small services, each running in its own process
and communicating with lightweight mechanisms (often an HTTP resource API),
built around business capabilities and independently deployable. Lists nine
characteristics (including decentralized governance and data management, smart
endpoints and dumb pipes, infrastructure automation, design for failure) and
discusses the tradeoffs of the style against the monolith. Web-verified: article
published 2014-03-25 at martinfowler.com; secondary sources (DreamFactory
whitepaper; Oregon State "Handbook of Software Engineering Methods"; ThoughtWorks
blog) confirm the definition, the nine characteristics, and the date.

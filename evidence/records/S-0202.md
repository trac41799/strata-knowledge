Classic graduate-level textbook on distributed database systems (fourth
edition, 2020). Defines the field as the union of data distribution
(fragmentation/partitioning), replication, distributed query processing and
optimization, and distributed transaction management; covers horizontal and
vertical fragmentation, distributed join strategies (e.g., semijoin, partition
join), and transaction coordination. The fourth edition adds new chapters on
big data platforms and on NoSQL, NewSQL and polystore systems. Web-verified:
Springer product page (link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-030-26253-2) and
author site (cs.uwaterloo.ca/~ddbook).

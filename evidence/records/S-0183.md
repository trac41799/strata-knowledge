Codd's normalization paper (presented at the Courant Symposium, New
York, May 24-25, 1971; IBM Research Report RJ909). Formalizes
functional dependencies and defines the first, second, and third
normal forms as properties of relations relative to their dependency
sets, and shows how relations can be decomposed into normal-form
relations. The origin of 2NF and 3NF; the definitional basis for
subsequent formal treatments (e.g., Bernstein 1976). Web-verified:
venue and page range per Wikipedia "Database normalization" citations
and ACM/NAS biographical sources.

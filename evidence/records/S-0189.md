The standard survey of query evaluation. Describes the iterative
(pull-based) execution of complex query evaluation plans over operator
trees, and systematically covers the join algorithms — nested-loop
variants (simple, block, index) and the duality of sort-merge and
hash-based set-matching algorithms (hash join variants, partition
handling for memory limits) — with cost and complexity analyses, plus
parallel execution and special operators. Web-verified: ACM Digital
Library DOI 10.1145/152610.152611; ACM Computing Surveys 25(2):
73-170 per DBLP.

Canonical simplification of the Paxos consensus protocol (originally "The
Part-Time Parliament", 1998). Establishes the two-phase prepare/promise →
accept structure, the safety argument via quorum intersection, and the
orthogonality of leader election. Primary source for Paxos claims
(`systems-software/distributed-consensus`).

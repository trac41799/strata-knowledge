Large-N industrial survey (level 5) of software maintenance across 487 data
processing organizations — the classic empirical anchor for the cost-share and
maintenance-mix claims. Web-verified via secondary citations in peer-reviewed
and standards literature: an IEEE/ISO survey literature review by A. Abran et
al. (Otto-von-Guericke University, publicationslist.org/data/a.abran/ref-2196)
reports that Lientz & Swanson found 55% of maintenance requests were new
requirements rather than corrections; Kemerer & Slaughter ("Toward a Detailed
Classification Scheme for Software Maintenance Requests", University of
Pittsburgh) report that Lientz & Swanson found most — at least 50% — of
maintenance effort was perfective (p. 68); NBS/GovInfo guidance cites the study
as a cornerstone of maintenance problem analysis. Basis for the
maintenance-is-mostly-enhancement and maintenance-effort-share claims of
`operations/software-maintenance`.

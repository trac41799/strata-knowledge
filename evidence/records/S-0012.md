Three-stage model of skill acquisition: cognitive (understanding what to do),
associative (practicing and connecting), autonomous (automatic execution).
Basis for Strata's learner-stage placement (novice → advanced-beginner →
competent → proficient → expert), which adapts teaching strategy per stage.

Canonical computational-theory textbook: Turing machines and the Church–Turing
thesis, decidable vs Turing-recognizable vs co-recognizable languages
(a language is decidable iff it and its complement are recognizable),
many-one (mapping) reductions and complete problems, and Rice's theorem.
Widely adopted course text; basis for the taxonomy and practice-level (T3)
claims of this topic.

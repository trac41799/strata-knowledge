Verified from publisher record and paper: frames function estimation as
numerical optimization in function space rather than parameter space, and
derives gradient boosting as a steepest-descent (gradient-descent) paradigm
for stagewise additive expansions under any differentiable fitting criterion.
Specific algorithms are given for least-squares, least-absolute-deviation and
Huber-M losses for regression and multiclass logistic likelihood for
classification, including the special case where the additive components are
regression trees ("TreeBoost"). The paper states that gradient boosting of
regression trees produces competitive, highly robust procedures, especially
for "less than clean data". Canonical source for boosting-style ensemble
practice (sequential bias reduction by fitting weak learners to residuals).

The canonical database-systems textbook. Covers physical storage
systems (storage tiers, the buffer manager/buffer pool and its
replacement policies, slotted-page record layout), indexing (B+-trees
and their height/insertion analysis, hash indexes, clustered vs
secondary indexes), log-structured storage and LSM-style structures,
and transaction processing: ACID properties, isolation anomalies
(including lost updates), the SQL isolation levels and their locking
implementations, multiversion concurrency control and snapshot
isolation (including write skew), two-phase locking, and recovery via
write-ahead logging.

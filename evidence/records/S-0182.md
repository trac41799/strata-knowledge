The founding paper of the relational model (CACM, June 1970). Proposes
viewing all data in a data bank as a collection of time-varying n-ary
relations defined on domains, with data addressed by content rather
than by physical position or access path (data independence). Defines
keys (sets of attributes identifying tuples), discusses normalization
to remove redundancy, and introduces the fundamental operations —
selection, projection, and joins (including the join expressed as
natural composition of relations) — as set-at-a-time operations over
relations. Web-verified: ACM Digital Library DOI 10.1145/362384.362685.

Codified consensus for requirements engineering: defines requirements
engineering (clause 5.2) as the interdisciplinary function that mediates
between acquirer and developer/supplier domains to establish and maintain
the requirements of the system, software, or service of interest, covering
discovering, eliciting, developing, analyzing, verifying, validating,
communicating, documenting, and managing requirements. Defines
characteristics of a good individual requirement (necessary, appropriate,
unambiguous, complete, singular, feasible, verifiable, correct) and of a
good set of requirements (complete, consistent, feasible, comprehensible,
traceable). Provides requirements-related processes and information-item
guidance aligned with ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207 and 15288, applied iteratively and
recursively across the life cycle; replaced IEEE 830-1998. Confirmed current
in 2024; a revision (DIS 29148) is in progress as of 2026.

The origin of the STRIDE model: defines Spoofing, Tampering with data,
Repudiability, Information disclosure (privacy breach), Denial of Service, and
Elevation of privilege, with example vulnerabilities per category; defines
threat / vulnerability / attack; positions threat identification as the first
step of proactive (design-phase) security analysis; notes that a system is
only as strong as its weakest link and that threats left unaddressed must be
identified and rationalized. Authoritative source for the STRIDE claims of the
`security/threat-modeling` topic.

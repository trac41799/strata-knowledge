The foundational quantum threat result: Shor's algorithm solves integer
factorization and discrete logarithms in polynomial time on a quantum
computer, using polynomially many quantum operations together with polynomial
classical post-processing. The algorithm reduces both problems to
period-finding, solved by the quantum Fourier transform. Because RSA's
security rests on the difficulty of factoring and Diffie-Hellman/elliptic-
curve cryptography on the discrete logarithm problem, a large-scale,
fault-tolerant quantum computer implementing Shor's algorithm would break
them. Original version appeared at the 35th Annual Symposium on Foundations
of Computer Science (FOCS 1994); expanded journal version in SIAM Journal on
Computing 26(5), 1997, pp. 1484-1509 (also reprinted in SIAM Review 41(2),
1999, pp. 303-332). Verified against arXiv:quant-ph/9508027 (journal
reference) and the SIAM Review reprint.

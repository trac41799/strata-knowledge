Presents PLONK, a universal zk-SNARK with fully succinct verification and an
updatable universal structured reference string (SRS), removing the
per-circuit trusted-setup obstacle of earlier pairing-based SNARKs. Works on
polynomial evaluations over a subgroup (Lagrange basis) with a permutation
argument, and commits to the circuit's polynomials using the polynomial
commitment scheme of Kate, Zaverucha and Goldberg (KZG, ASIACRYPT 2010), which
yields constant-size commitments and evaluation proofs verified with a few
pairing equations. Reports the prover running time is roughly 7.5-20x lower
in group exponentiations than the fully succinct mode of its predecessor
Sonic (Maller et al., CCS 2019), depending on circuit structure. Verified
against the IACR ePrint 2019/953 page.

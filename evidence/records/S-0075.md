Influential practitioner article (IEEE Computer, Jan 2001). Item 1: "Finding
and fixing a software problem after delivery is often 100 times more
expensive than finding and fixing it during the requirements and design
phase" — a large-system finding; escalation is closer to ~5:1 for smaller,
less critical systems, and exact per-phase multipliers are contested in later
critiques. The direction (late fixes cost more than early ones) is consistent
across the studies the authors summarize, and the article is the canonical
citation for the cost-of-defects argument in requirements engineering.

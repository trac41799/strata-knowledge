Internet Standard for the JWT claims format: a compact, URL-safe representation of
claims (a JSON payload) carried inside a JWS (signed) or JWE (encrypted) structure.
Registered claim names: iss, sub, aud, exp, nbf, iat, jti (Sec. 4.1); tokens MUST
NOT be accepted at or after their exp time, and a receiver MUST identify itself in
aud when present or reject the token. Unsecured JWTs ("alg":"none") are defined
(Sec. 6); validation MUST reject JWTs whose algorithms are not understood or
acceptable to the application (Sec. 7.2), and implementations MUST support both
HS256 and "none" (Sec. 8) — so verifiers must explicitly enforce an algorithm
allow-list. Trust decisions must not rely on JWT contents unless cryptographically
secured and bound to the decision context (Sec. 11.1); privacy-sensitive claims
require encryption or TLS in transit (Sec. 12).

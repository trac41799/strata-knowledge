Google experience report on its continuous testing platform (TAP): in a
15-month window, 4.56% of 1.6M test failures (~73,000) were attributed to
flaky tests. Describes Google's infrastructure for triaging flaky failures
(test labeling, rerunning flaky tests until a passing run is obtained) and
the operational machinery (test scheduling, prioritization, result
distillation) needed to keep CI feedback fast at scale.

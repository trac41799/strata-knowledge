Trace-simulation study of shared-memory multiprocessor workloads (ASPLOS-IV
1991 origin). Defines and measures false-sharing misses versus true-sharing
misses; shows multiprocessor cache miss rates do not drop with larger blocks as
uniprocessors do, and that false sharing and spatial locality in shared data
measurably affect performance. Quantifies false sharing as a coherence artifact
of block granularity rather than real data sharing.

Seventh annual State of DevOps report. Benchmarks organizations as elite,
high, medium, or low performers via cluster analysis on delivery metrics
(deployment frequency, lead time for changes, time to restore service,
change failure rate; reliability examined separately). 2021 findings: elite
performers deploy on demand (multiple times per day), report lead time for
changes under one hour, change failure rate 0–15% (mean 7.5% vs 23% for low
performers) and restore service in under one hour; relative to low
performers, elite performers show ~973x more frequent deployments, ~6,570x
faster lead time and recovery, and ~3x lower change failure rate. Also
measures continuous-delivery technical practices (loosely coupled
architecture, trunk-based development, continuous testing, continuous
integration, deployment automation, monitoring and observability practices,
management of database changes) and shift-left security practices.

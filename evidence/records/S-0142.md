The "Gang of Four" catalog: 23 object-oriented design patterns classified by
purpose into creational (5), structural (7), and behavioral (11) families.
Defines the pattern concept via four essential elements — name, problem,
solution, consequences — and stresses that a pattern describes a solution to
be adapted, not a finished design transformable directly into code. Includes
selection guidance (examine causes of redesign, study consequences) and the
explicit caveat that "design patterns should not be applied indiscriminately"
because they buy flexibility by adding indirection. Web-verified: Addison-
Wesley, October 1994, ISBN 0-201-63361-2 (publisher, Wikipedia, Illinois
Experts bibliography); the "applied indiscriminately" caveat quoted in
secondary sources.

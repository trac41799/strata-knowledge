Introduces the Fiat-Shamir transform (heuristic): a public-coin interactive
proof of knowledge is converted into a non-interactive proof / signature by
replacing the verifier's random challenges with a hash of the transcript.
Motivating application is identification -- proving knowledge of a secret
without transmitting it -- and digital signatures built from it. The schemes
are proven secure against known/chosen-message attacks under the assumption
that factoring is difficult, and typical implementations require only 1/4 to
1 of the number of modular multiplications required by RSA. Presented at
CRYPTO 1986; proceedings published 1987. Verified against doi.org resolution
and the CRYPTO '86 proceedings record.

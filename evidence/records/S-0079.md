Codified consensus for IEEE members' professional conduct: 10 tenets under
three commitments — (I) uphold the highest standards of integrity,
responsible behavior, and ethical conduct; (II) treat all persons fairly and
with respect, without harassment or discrimination; (III) strive to ensure
the code is upheld by colleagues and co-workers. Tenets include: hold
paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public; ethical design and
sustainable development practices; protect the privacy of others; disclose
promptly factors that might endanger the public or the environment; improve
societal understanding of conventional and emerging technologies including
intelligent systems; avoid and disclose real or perceived conflicts of
interest; avoid unlawful conduct and reject bribery; honest claims and
estimates with proper credit to others; maintain technical competence and
undertake tasks only when qualified or after disclosing limitations; and not
retaliate against individuals reporting a violation.

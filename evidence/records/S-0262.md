Introduces random forests: ensembles of decision trees grown on bootstrap
samples with random feature subsets at each split, whose predictions are
averaged. Verified from the paper and publisher record: the generalization
error of a forest converges almost surely to a limit as the number of trees
grows — more trees do not overfit; accuracy depends on the strength and
correlation of the individual trees; the method is presented as working well
with few tuning parameters and as robust to noise. Canonical empirical and
theoretical basis for bagging-style ensemble practice (variance reduction by
averaging decorrelated high-variance base learners).

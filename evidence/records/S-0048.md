Standard undergraduate textbook for discrete mathematics: sets, relations and
functions, induction, combinatorics, graph theory, and modular arithmetic are
each developed with formal definitions and proofs (Chapters 2, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10
in the eighth edition). Widely adopted reference (in use at major CS programs
for three decades); source for the definitional claims of
`cs-foundations/discrete-mathematics`.

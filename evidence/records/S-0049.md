Foundational paper of Ramsey theory: proves that for any coloring of the
r-element subsets of a sufficiently large finite set with k colors there is a
monochromatic subset of prescribed size — a formal generalization of the
pigeonhole principle (the r=1 case). Also derives the graph version for pairs
(Ramsey numbers R(s,t)). Primary source for the pigeonhole/Ramsey claims of
`cs-foundations/discrete-mathematics`.

Quantitative synthesis of 839 assessments of distributed (spaced) vs massed
practice: spacing reliably improves recall, and the optimal gap grows with the
desired retention interval (roughly 10–20% of the retention interval). Basis for
Strata's review ladder (1/3/7/14/30/60/120 days).

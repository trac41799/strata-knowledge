Canonical textbook of the logic-programming paradigm: facts and rules,
unification and resolution, goal-directed deduction, backtracking and
nondeterminism, the relational (multi-directional) reading of predicates, and
the standard ISO Prolog dialect. Basis for the T3 claims on logic programming
in the programming-paradigms topic.

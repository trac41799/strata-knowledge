Proves the completeness theorem for first-order logic: every valid formula of
the classical predicate calculus is derivable in the standard axiom system, and
every satisfiable set of formulas has a model. With the converse direction
(soundness, checked directly on the axioms), this establishes that first-order
validity and provability coincide (Gamma ⊨ phi iff Gamma ⊢ phi); compactness
follows as a corollary. Formal basis for the soundness/completeness and
propositional/first-order claims of this topic.

Reevaluation of Amdahl's law: Amdahl's bound assumes the problem size is
fixed, but in practice problem size scales with the number of processors
(the paper's measurements on a 1024-processor hypercube achieved near-linear
speedups). Derives Gustafson's (scaled) speedup law: with total execution
time held fixed and serial fraction s (of the scaled run), speedup equals
s + N(1-s), which grows with N rather than saturating at 1/s. Shows the two
laws answer different questions: fixed-size speedup (Amdahl) vs fixed-time
scaled speedup (Gustafson). Basis for the T0 Gustafson's-law claims of
`systems-software/parallel-programming`.

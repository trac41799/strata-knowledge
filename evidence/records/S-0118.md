For a uniprocessor with preemptive scheduling of independent periodic
tasks with deadlines equal to periods, proves that earliest-deadline-first
(EDF) is optimal among all scheduling algorithms — a task set is EDF-
schedulable iff total utilization ≤ 1 — and that rate-monotonic scheduling
is optimal among fixed-priority schedulers, with the feasibility bound
Σ(Cᵢ/Tᵢ) ≤ n(2^(1/n) − 1).

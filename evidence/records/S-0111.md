Codified consensus defining traditional NAT: address/port translation at a gateway
enabling many private hosts to share a single public address (basic NAT and
NAPT variants), and its documented side effect of breaking IP's end-to-end
transparency model. Authoritative source for the NAT claim in
`systems-software/networking-basics`.

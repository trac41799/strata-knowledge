Formal derivation that, on a single machine, sequencing jobs in non-
decreasing processing time — the shortest-processing-time (SPT) rule, i.e.,
shortest job first — minimizes mean flow time, and therefore average
waiting time, via an interchange/exchange argument. This is the proof of
SJF optimality for average waiting time.

Defines the MESIF protocol: five stable states M, E, S, I, and Forward (F).
The F state designates a single "first-among-equals" sharer responsible for
supplying the line's data on read requests, enabling two-hop responses on
point-to-point interconnects; basis of the Intel QuickPath Interconnect (QPI)
coherence protocol.

The book-length synthesis of the DevOps Research and Assessment (DORA) survey
program: four years of large-scale industry survey data on software delivery
performance. Defines the four key delivery metrics — deployment frequency,
lead time for changes, time to restore service (MTTR), and change failure
rate — and the model of 24 capabilities in five categories (continuous
delivery, architecture, product & process, lean management & monitoring,
culture) that predict delivery performance. The canonical citation for the
DORA/Accelerate metric definitions (survey-based observational research,
not an experiment; benchmarks in later DORA reports supersede its point
estimates).

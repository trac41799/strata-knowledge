Systematic survey of a decade of mutation testing research (2010s). Frames
mutation testing as the use of artificial defects to evaluate test adequacy;
reports mutation-based adequacy as among the most effective yet most
computationally expensive criteria, with the main cost-reduction strategies
being selective mutation, weak mutation, mutant sampling, and equivalent-
mutant detection; covers mutation-based test generation and fault
localization. Adopted in practice at industrial scale (e.g., Google) and
supported by a growing tool ecosystem.

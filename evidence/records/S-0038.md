Defines sequential consistency (SC): "the result of any execution is the same as
if the operations of all the processors were executed in some sequential order,
and the operations of each individual processor appear in this sequence in the
order specified by its program." Formalizes the correctness contract between
multiprocessor hardware and multiprocess programs; SC is a consistency model,
not a coherence protocol.

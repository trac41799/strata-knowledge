The RED method: for every service, monitor the request Rate, the Error rate
of those requests, and a Duration (latency) distribution — giving teams a
consistent per-service instrumentation template ("everyone should understand
the error rate, the request rate, and then some distribution of latency for
those requests… putting people on call for code they didn't write"). The
treatment positions RED against Brendan Gregg's USE method (Utilization,
Saturation, Errors for every resource) and against the four golden signals,
with RED as the request-oriented view for services and USE as the
resource-oriented view for infrastructure.

Foundational formalization of transactions and consistency: defines
transactions, schedules, and consistency constraints, and proves the
two-phase locking theorem — if all transactions obey the two-phase rule
(no lock released before every lock required by the transaction is
acquired) and requests are granted compatibly, then any schedule
executed by the system is serializable (equivalent to a serial
execution). Also introduces locking granularity and predicate locks.
This is the proof basis for 2PL-as-serializability claims.

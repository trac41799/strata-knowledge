Standard graduate architecture textbook (ACM A.M. Turing Award winners,
2017). Chapter 2 covers memory hierarchy design: SRAM/DRAM/Flash technology,
cache organizations (sets, ways, blocks, index formula), write policies, the
3C miss model, AMAT = hit time + miss rate × miss penalty, and worked
4.0 GHz CPU worked examples with concrete numbers (L1 hit = 4 cycles; DDR4-2400 DRAM
latency ≈ 40 ns ≈ 160 cycles at 4 GHz; total miss penalty ≈ 200 cycles).
Appendix K surveys instruction set architectures. ISBN 978-0-12-811905-1.

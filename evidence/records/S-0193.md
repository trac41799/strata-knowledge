The classic compendium of transaction-management practice ("folklore")
that codified the standard model for database operating systems:
locking-based concurrency control with lock granularity, degrees of
consistency (the ancestor of SQL isolation levels), recovery via
logging (write-ahead undo/redo), and the two-phase commit (2PC)
protocol for distributed transactions, including its failure behavior:
a participant that has voted to commit cannot unilaterally abort, so
coordinator failure leaves participants blocked in an uncertain state.

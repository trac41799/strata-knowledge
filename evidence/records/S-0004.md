Authoritative utility rating of 10 learning techniques: HIGH — practice testing,
distributed practice; MODERATE — interleaved practice, elaborative interrogation,
self-explanation; LOW — rereading, highlighting, summarization, keyword mnemonics,
imagery. Strata's pedagogy (Layer 2, `validation.md` contract) is built on this
ranking.

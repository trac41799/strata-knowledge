First extensive empirical study of flaky tests: analysis of 201 commits
fixing flaky tests across 51 open-source projects. Classifies root causes —
asynchronous waits (~45%), concurrency (~20%), test-order dependency (~12%),
plus network, time, and I/O causes — and documents developer fix strategies
(adding waits, fixing ordering, isolation). Establishes the definition of a
flaky test as one that passes and fails on the same code, and motivates
test-isolation practices in automated suites.

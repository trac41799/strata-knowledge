Peer-reviewed industrial study of Facebook's production memcached fleet
(billions of requests per second, trillions of items). Introduces leases to
solve two cache-consistency problems: stale sets (concurrent reordered
updates install an outdated value) and thundering herds (a key under heavy
read/write activity is repeatedly invalidated, so many reads fall through to
the more costly backing store). Lease tokens are issued at a regulated rate
(default once per 10 seconds per key); waiting clients are notified rather
than allowed to hit the store. Documents delete-based invalidation, memcache
pools separating high-churn and low-churn keys, and an evaluation in which
leases reduced a contended key's peak database query rate from roughly
17,000/s to 1,300/s.

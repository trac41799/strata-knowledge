Item 1 apply formative Q3

Write your answer first — retrieval before reading (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006).

At 12:00:00 a proxy stores a response with Date: 12:00:00, Age: 0, Cache-Control: public, max-age=300. The proxy forwards it at 12:03:00. What Age value must the proxy send, and is the response still fresh?

Model answer · evidence S-0009

Age = 180 (3 minutes of resident time added to prior Age 0). Freshness lifetime is 300 s, current age 180 s < 300 s, so it is still fresh and may be served without contacting the origin.

Item 2 remember formative Q1

Multiple choice · distractors constructed from claims in concept.md.

Which single Cache-Control directive truly prevents a response from being stored and reused by any cache?

Model answer · evidence S-0009

no-store. no-cache still allows storage (it only forces revalidation before reuse), and private still allows the browser’s own cache to store the response.

Session close

When both items are graded, the session would log quiz.attempted with per-Bloom scores and schedule the next review — that bookkeeping is the harness’s job, not this folio’s.

The ladder

Distributed practice, per Cepeda et al. (2006): 1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 → 60 → 120 days, optimal gap roughly 10–20% of the retention interval.

Each completed review advances the rung; the shelf re-evaluates on every visit, so an overdue item sits at the top, quietly.

¶ Provenance

Both due items, intervals, and review counts come verbatim from the committed example queue.

journey/examples/review-queue.example.json

¶ Overdue, not failing

The brick ink marks a missed date, nothing more. Reviews are never a punishment — the ladder simply resumes where it left off.

¶ Interleaving

Sessions mix this topic with its prerequisites, so the shelf double-checks the foundation each time it climbs.

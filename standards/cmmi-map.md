# CMMI V3.0 — Standards Map

Capability Maturity Model Integration V3.0 (ISACA / CMMI Institute, released April 2023).
8 domains, 31 Practice Areas (17 core applying across all domains + 14 domain-specific).
Topic ids below are the developer-learning topics that cover each Practice Area's intent.

## Domains

- Development (DEV)
- Services (SVC)
- Supplier Management (SPM)
- Data Management (DAT)
- People Management (PPL)
- Security (SEC)
- Safety (SAF)
- Virtual Work (VIR)

## Core Practice Areas

- `engineering-process/requirements-engineering` — RDM (Requirements Development and Management): eliciting, analyzing, specifying requirements
- `engineering-process/requirements-traceability` — RDM: maintaining bidirectional traceability from need to solution
- `engineering-process/config-management` — CM (Configuration Management): identification, control, status accounting, audits
- `operations/release-management` — CM: controlled release and deployment of baselined configurations
- `quality-testing/peer-review` — PR (Peer Reviews): structured review of work products by peers
- `quality-testing/inspection-walkthrough` — PR: formal inspection and walkthrough techniques
- `engineering-process/project-planning` — PLAN (Planning): work breakdown, schedules, budgets, resource plans
- `engineering-process/work-breakdown-structure` — PLAN: decomposing work into manageable, estimable tasks
- `engineering-process/effort-estimation` — EST (Estimating): size, effort, duration, and cost estimation
- `engineering-process/project-monitoring-and-control` — MC (Monitor and Control): progress tracking and corrective action
- `engineering-process/risk-management` — RSK (Risk and Opportunity Management): risk identification, analysis, mitigation
- `engineering-process/governance` — GOV (Governance): management oversight, alignment with objectives, decision rights
- `engineering-process/process-definition` — II (Implementation Infrastructure): establishing process infrastructure and sustaining habits
- `engineering-process/competency-development` — OT (Organizational Training): skills development, training delivery, competency assessment
- `engineering-process/process-asset-library` — PAD (Process Asset Development): standard processes and reusable assets
- `engineering-process/process-improvement` — PCM (Process Management): managing and improving standard processes
- `engineering-process/process-tailoring` — PCM: tailoring standard processes to project context
- `quality-testing/process-quality-assurance` — PQA (Process Quality Assurance): objective evaluation of process compliance
- `engineering-process/quality-audit` — PQA: audits of work products and processes against standards
- `engineering-process/measurement-and-analysis` — MPM (Managing Performance and Measurement): measures, analysis, performance objectives
- `data/statistical-process-control` — MPM: statistical and quantitative techniques for high maturity
- `quality-testing/verification` — VV (Verification and Validation): verifying work products meet requirements
- `quality-testing/validation` — VV: validating that solutions meet intended use
- `quality-testing/root-cause-analysis` — CAR (Causal Analysis and Resolution): causal analysis and corrective/preventive action
- `engineering-process/decision-analysis` — DAR (Decision Analysis and Resolution): structured evaluation of alternatives

## Domain-Specific Practice Areas

- `architecture-design/technical-design` — TS (Technical Solution, Development): design, development, and implementation of solutions
- `engineering-process/product-integration` — PI (Product Integration, Development): assembling components and integrating the product
- `operations/business-continuity` — CONT (Continuity, Services): continuity planning for significant disruptions
- `operations/disaster-recovery` — CONT: recovery procedures and rehearsal of continuity plans
- `operations/incident-management` — IRP (Incident Resolution and Prevention, Services): timely resolution of service incidents
- `operations/problem-management` — IRP: prevention of recurring incidents through root-cause action
- `operations/service-delivery` — SDM (Service Delivery Management, Services): delivering services per service agreements
- `operations/service-strategy` — STSM (Strategic Service Management, Services): standard services aligned to strategic needs
- `engineering-process/supplier-management` — SAM (Supplier Agreement Management, Supplier Management): selecting suppliers and managing agreements
- `security/security-engineering` — ESEC (Enabling Security, Security): designing and maintaining secure systems
- `security/threat-modeling` — MST (Managing Security Threats and Vulnerabilities, Security): threat identification and analysis
- `security/vulnerability-management` — MST: vulnerability assessment, remediation, and monitoring
- `data/data-management` — DM (Data Management, Data Management): organizing and using data per business requirements
- `data/data-governance` — DM: data policies, ownership, and accountability
- `data/data-quality` — DQ (Data Quality, Data Management): accuracy, completeness, and fitness of data
- `engineering-process/safety-engineering` — ESAF (Enabling Safety, Safety): hazard analysis and safety-critical development
- `engineering-process/workforce-empowerment` — WE (Workforce Empowerment, People Management): empowering individuals and workgroups
- `operations/virtual-work` — EVW (Enabling Virtual Work, Virtual Work): enabling distributed and virtual workforce delivery
